      How it works

      No database. The chain is the ledger.

      1. SpawnSign one memo to the board. Your wallet becomes an NPC with a tag and a line.
      2. AcceptPick a task. Your accept is a memo on the board with the reward written in it.
      3. Do the workTrade, host a pool, catch a newborn coin or hold the line, from your own wallet.
      4. Get paidThe site reads your wallet's transactions. Done tasks are paid in SOL from the treasury.

      Questions

      Is this real SOL?

      Yes. Rewards are sent from the treasury wallet, which is funded by $NPC creator fees. Every payout is an on-chain transfer with an npc:pay memo you can check on Solscan.

      Does the site hold my keys or money?

      No. The server only builds transactions. Your wallet shows and signs each one. Spawning and accepting send 0 SOL; you pay only the network fee.

      How is a task checked?

      After you accept, the server reads your wallet's own transactions from Solana. A pump.fun or PumpSwap buy or sell completes Trader, a PumpSwap deposit completes Host, a buy within 30 minutes of a coin's graduation completes First responder, and your $NPC balance after 24 hours completes Hold the line.

      When do I get paid?

      A completed task is owed to you until the treasury sends the next payout batch. Your NPC shows every task as open, done, owed or paid, with links.

      Why can't I take a task?

      Each NPC can hold one of each task at a time, and a task cools down for 24 hours after you complete it. Hold the line opens when $NPC is live.

      Is NPC part of pump.fun?

      No. NPC is independent. It reads public pump.fun and PumpSwap activity from Solana.
