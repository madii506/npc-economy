      Questions

      Is this real SOL?

      Yes. Rewards are sent from the treasury wallet, which is funded by $NPC creator fees. Every payout is an on-chain transfer with an npc:pay memo you can check on Solscan.

      Does the site hold my keys or money?

      No. The server only builds transactions. Your wallet shows and signs each one. Spawning and accepting send 0 SOL; you pay only the network fee.

      How is a task checked?

      After you accept, the server reads your wallet's own transactions. A pump.fun or PumpSwap trade completes Trader, a PumpSwap deposit completes Host, a buy within 30 minutes of a graduation completes First responder, and your $NPC balance after 24 hours completes Hold the line.

      When do I get paid?

      A completed task is owed until the treasury sends the next payout batch. Your NPC shows every task as open, done, owed or paid.

      Why can't I take a task?

      One of each task at a time, and a 24 hour cooldown after you finish one. Hold the line opens when $NPC is live.

      What are ranks?

      A title from the number of tasks your NPC has finished on-chain. Ranks don't change rewards.

      Can I see someone else's NPC?

      Yes. Paste any wallet into the lookup above Your NPC. Everything is public on Solana anyway.

      Is NPC part of pump.fun?

      No. NPC is independent. It reads public pump.fun and PumpSwap activity from Solana.
